# Feature Goals

FeatureSpecs are untrusted bounded intake records, never Roadmaps or executable workflow authority. A Feature Goal persists exactly one canonical Task and `/goal` before Backend Engineer execution.

GoalRun provider sessions are isolated by GoalRun and role. They may be resumed only as disposable optimization; Laravel/AIOS remains authoritative for approval, Task lifecycle, validation, Git, review, audit, recovery, and all historical evidence. Legacy Coder, Roadmap, and Ticket flows retain their fresh-context contracts.
